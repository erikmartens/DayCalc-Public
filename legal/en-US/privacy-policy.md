## 1. Overview

DayCalc respects your privacy. The App provides calendar, deadline, and date-calculation features and processes your app-specific data primarily on your device.

## 2. Accounts, Tracking, and Advertising

DayCalc does not require a user account. The App does not sell personal data, use advertising identifiers, or create advertising or tracking profiles.

## 3. Local Data Storage

DayCalc may store the following information locally on your device:

- deadlines you create, including their title, due date, category, completion state, and selected reminders
- recent date-calculation results
- calendar visibility, appearance, language, and other app preferences
- technical state needed for the app installation and the one-time migration of existing DayCalc data

This app data is not uploaded to a DayCalc server for the App to operate.

## 4. Calendar Access and Export

If you grant calendar access, DayCalc reads the calendar, event, and calendar-metadata values needed by the selected features through Apple's EventKit framework. Calendar data remains in the iOS-managed calendar store; DayCalc does not copy the entire calendar store into its own persistent database.

When you expressly export an event or deadline to your calendar, DayCalc gives the values you confirm to EventKit. Calendar accounts and synchronized calendar services are managed by Apple or the relevant account provider and are subject to their privacy terms. You can change calendar permission at any time in iOS Settings.

## 5. Local Notifications

If you enable reminders, DayCalc may schedule local notifications for your deadlines. Notification requests and their content are managed on the device by iOS. DayCalc does not use remote push notifications for this feature. You can change notification permission at any time in iOS Settings.

## 6. Diagnostics

DayCalc may create bounded technical diagnostics locally to handle errors and migration of existing data safely. The current app configuration does not send analytics or crash reports to the developer. Sensitive calendar or deadline content is not intentionally included in diagnostics.

## 7. Support and External Services

If you contact support by email or through GitHub, your message and any information you voluntarily attach are processed by the relevant external service. Do not include information you do not want to share.

DayCalc may open websites, email applications, GitHub, or iOS system interfaces. These external services are outside DayCalc's control and are governed by their own privacy terms.

## 8. Retention and Deletion

You can remove app-owned deadlines, calculation history, and preferences through available App controls. Deleting the App removes app data stored locally by DayCalc, subject to normal iOS backup behavior. Events exported to Apple Calendar are not automatically deleted and must be managed in Calendar.

## 9. Security

You are responsible for protecting your device and connected calendar accounts with available system security such as a passcode, Touch ID, or Face ID. No storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 10. Children

DayCalc is not directed specifically to children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## 11. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when features or data handling change. The current version will be made available in the App and in the public legal documents.
